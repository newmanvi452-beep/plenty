Hosting should feel like a party, not a project. Serves 20 turns "how many people are coming?" into a menu, a shopping list and a cooking plan, so the big day goes smoothly.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tell Serves 20 how many adults and kids are coming and what time you're eating.
• Pick dishes from the menu. Serves 20 suggests how many of each kind you need and scales every recipe to your guest count.
• Get one combined shopping list, sorted by aisle, with everything you need from every dish. Check things off as you shop.
• Follow a cooking plan for the days before and a countdown for the big day, so everything lands on the table together.

LET GUESTS HELP
• Share a link and guests can sign up to bring a dish, drinks or paper plates. No account needed for them.
• See who is bringing what at a glance, and take anyone off the list if plans change. Dishes others bring come off your shopping list.

MADE FOR REAL KITCHENS
• Thanksgiving mode and a more casual party mode, for any gathering.
• Add your own recipes by typing them in or taking a photo of a recipe card.
• Notes for every event, so next year you remember what worked.
• Works offline, so the plan is there even when the kitchen Wi-Fi isn't.
• Sign in with just your email (no password) to keep your plans on every device.

ONE PRICE
